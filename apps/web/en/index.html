Electric vehicle charging

Every charger. One app.

Find the nearest charger, see its power, plugs and price when the operator publishes it, then pay the operator directly from Branché: the charger’s verified QR code or official payment page, with no account and no app to install.

How it works

Three steps, whoever operates the charger.

  1. 1

    Find

    The map opens on your location and shows the chargers around you, nearest first.

  2. 2

    Choose

    Power, plugs, access and price when the operator publishes it: see at a glance whether the charger suits your car.

  3. 3

    Pay the operator

    Scan the charger’s QR code or tap “Charge here”: Branché checks that the payment page really belongs to the operator and opens it, no account needed. The operator takes the payment and starts the charge.

Transparency

What Branché does, and does not do

The chargers shown are real: they come from France’s national IRVE database published on data.gouv.fr. Branché takes no money: you pay the charger’s operator, on its verified official page or by bank card at the charger when it accepts one.

  • Public data, sources cited
  • No account, nothing charged by Branché
  • Operator payment pages verified before opening
  • Card payment at the charger shown when available
